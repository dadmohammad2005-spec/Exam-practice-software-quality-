# 🎓 SQE Master — Software Quality Engineering Exam Browser

A dedicated, offline-first **Exam Browser & MCQ Practice Platform** built specifically for your Software Quality Engineering (SQE) exam preparation, powered by all **245 verified Multiple-Choice Questions** extracted directly from your course materials.

---

## 🚀 How to Launch

You can run the Exam Browser instantly using either method:

### Method 1: One-Click Windows Launcher (Fastest)
Double-click:
`Launch_Exam_Browser.bat`

### Method 2: Direct Browser Open
Double-click or drag-and-drop:
`index.html` into Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Brave.

*No web server, node, or python backend required — everything runs 100% locally and offline in your browser.*

## ✨ Features Built for Your Preparation

### 1. ⏱️ Official Exam Simulation Mode
- **Authentic Kiosk-Style Exam Environment**: Fullscreen mode, distraction-free interface.
- **45-Minute Countdown Timer**: Visual and subtle audio alerts when under 5 minutes remain.
- **Question Palette**: Interactive navigation grid showing Unvisited, Answered, and Flagged (Marked for Review) questions.
- **Submission Guard**: Confirmation modal summarizing your answered, unanswered, and flagged questions before submitting.
- **Comprehensive Score Card**:
  - Score percentage, Pass/Fail status (Passing mark: 60%).
  - Time spent and overall accuracy.
  - Topic-by-topic performance bars.
  - In-depth Review Mode showing your chosen answer vs the correct answer with full teacher notes.

### 2. 💡 Interactive Practice Mode (Immediate Feedback)
- Instant visual feedback on click:
  - **Correct answer**: Green highlight and checkmark.
  - **Incorrect answer**: Red highlight with error cue, while instantly revealing the correct answer.
- **Auto-revealed Explanations**: Detailed reasoning with source slide citations.
- Filter practice sessions by specific topic/module or PDF source.

### 3. 🎯 Mistakes Revision Vault (Targeted Weakness Fixing)
- Automatically tracks every question you answer incorrectly across any exam or practice session.
- Allows you to launch a dedicated "Fix Mistakes" drill focusing **only** on questions you struggled with until you reach 100% mastery.

### 4. ⭐ Starred / Bookmarked Questions
- Click the star icon (☆) on any tricky or high-yield question to save it for quick last-minute revision before the exam.

### 5. 🔍 Searchable Question Bank & Printable Cram Sheet
- Live instant search across all 245 questions, options, and explanations.
- Filter by topic, source, or status (Mistakes, Starred, Mastered).
- **"Print Cram Sheet"** button: Formats all questions and answers into a clean, printable summary for offline study.

### 6. ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
- `1` / `2` / `3` / `4` or `A` / `B` / `C` / `D` — Select options
- `→` or `N` — Next question
- `←` or `P` — Previous question
- `F` or `M` — Flag / Mark for review
- `C` — Clear selection
- `?` — Open Keyboard Shortcuts Dialog
- `Esc` — Close dialogs

### 7. 🎨 Sensory & Visual Polish
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Toggle between sleek dark slate and crisp academic light theme.
- **Pure Web Audio Effects**: Pleasant non-intrusive sound effects for clicks, correct answers, and warnings (easily muted with the bell button).
- **LocalStorage Persistence**: Automatically saves your exam history, bookmarks, mistakes, and settings on your computer.
